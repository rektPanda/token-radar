How the value is built

One function scores every protocol. Nothing in it names a protocol: the same arithmetic ranks the whole board.

implied = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × breadth × category

growth    = 1 + clamp(7-day change, −60%, +120%) ÷ 100 × 0.8
maturity  = 0.5 + 0.5 × min(age in days, 1460) ÷ 1460
breadth   = 1 + 0.05 × min(chains, 5)
category  = 0.9 + 0.2 × (category TVL ÷ largest category TVL)

Who gets ranked

A protocol is ranked when its record carries no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol), and it is not an exchange, a versioned pool, or a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. The watchlist is the floor: all 40 of its protocols are on the board, and the board adds every other protocol that passes the same rule, at any TVL.

What the number is not

The implied value is this entry’s own claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
